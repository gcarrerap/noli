## Zib — El extraterrestre

**Rasgos fijos**

- about the size of a house cat (30 cm)
- round jelly-bean-shaped body, a little wider at the bottom, no neck
- soft lavender-purple skin (#A98BE0), slightly glossy, with a lighter lilac belly (#D9CCF5)
- two big round glossy navy eyes (#1C1A3A) with tiny star-shaped sparkles
- rosy cheeks, small smiling mouth, no teeth
- two tiny stubby arms with three fingers each, NO legs
- three thin springy antennae on top of its head, each ending in a small glowing bulb
- floats a few inches above the ground with a faint lime glow underneath

**Ropa:** No clothes. Sometimes borrows things: a too-big pirate hat (ch. 3).

**Disfraz:** On Halloween night it goes 'dressed as Zib' (no costume) with a tiny orange candy bucket.

**Expresiones:** Antenna bulbs change color with its mood: yellow = a glow-seed is close (antennae point toward it), pink = happy or shy, blue = excited, gray and drooping = sad, rainbow = very happy. Bubbly, bobbing, sometimes floats upside down.

**Nunca:** never green skin; always exactly two eyes and exactly three antennae; never has legs, wings or teeth; must not look like E.T., minions, Mike Wazowski or any existing character.

**prompt_base:**

> Zib, a small cute alien about the size of a house cat with a round jelly-bean-shaped lavender-purple body, a lighter lilac belly, two big round glossy navy eyes with tiny star sparkles, rosy cheeks, two tiny three-fingered arms, no legs, and three thin springy antennae with small glowing bulbs, floating a few inches above the ground
