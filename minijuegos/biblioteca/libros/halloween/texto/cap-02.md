# Chapter 2: Hello-Treat!

Mari did not run away.

Her knees felt like jelly, and her heart went *bump, bump, bump*. But she did not run away.

"Let's find out," she said. That is what Mari always said when something was new.

She walked back to the pumpkin. The little window blinked green. Blink. Blink. Blink.

Then there was a sound like a soda can opening.

*Pssssst!*

The line around the middle of the pumpkin got wider. The top half of the pumpkin lifted up, slowly, like a lid on a pot.

A puff of mist came out. It smelled like oranges and peppermint.

Pepper made a sound like a squeaky toy and ran into the garage.

---

<!-- ilustracion: The pumpkin spaceship opening like a lid with soft mist and green light pouring out; Zib, a small round lavender alien with two big shiny eyes and three wobbly antennae with glowing tips, peeking over the edge; Mari in her helmet leaning forward, amazed. -->

Something peeked over the edge of the pumpkin.

It was round. It was about as big as a cat. It was a soft purple color, like a grape, and it was a little bit shiny.

It had two big eyes. They were dark and sparkly, like the night sky.

On top of its head were three thin antennae. Each one wobbled like a spring, and each one had a tiny light on the end.

The little creature looked at Mari. Mari looked at the little creature.

Then the creature floated up, out of the pumpkin. It did not have wings. It just floated, a little bit above the ground, and bobbed up and down like a balloon.

---

The creature floated closer. It looked at Mari's rain boots. It looked at her pajamas. Then it looked up at her shiny silver helmet.

Its antennae lit up bright blue.

"Oh!" said the creature. Its voice was high and bubbly, like a bird and a kazoo at the same time. "You are an astronaut! A real astronaut!"

Mari opened her mouth. Then she closed it. Then she opened it again.

"You can talk," she said.

"I can talk-talk-talk," said the creature. "I learn your words from the radio. On my ship. All the way here. Very long trip. Many songs about love and also about cars."

---

The creature bowed in the air. It turned all the way upside down and then right side up.

"My name is Zib," it said. "Hello-treat!"

"Hello-treat?" said Mari.

"Yes! On the radio, everybody says it. This week, very much. Trick-or-treat, trick-or-treat. It is a hello, yes? I say it nice. Hello-treat!"

Mari laughed. She could not help it. "It's not really a hello," she said. "I'll tell you about it later. My name is Mari."

"Mari," said Zib. Its antennae turned pink. "Mari, the astronaut."

"Well," said Mari, and she pushed her helmet up so she could see better. "I am not really an astronaut. Not yet. This is a costume. I made it out of a box."

---

<!-- ilustracion: Zib floating upside down in front of Mari, antennae glowing pink and blue; Mari laughing with her cardboard helmet pushed up; in the background Pepper's tail sticking out of a rain boot by the garage door. -->

Zib floated around Mari in a circle. It looked at the helmet very closely. It tapped the silver tape with one tiny purple hand.

"A box," said Zib. "You make a space helmet from a box. You are very smart human-friend."

Mari felt her cheeks get warm. Nobody had ever called her a very smart human-friend before.

"Where are you from?" she asked.

Zib pointed up, past the trees, past the clouds.

"Far," it said. "Very far. A purple planet with green rings. You cannot see it now. Too much sun. But it is there."

Just then, Pepper came out of the garage. She saw Zib. Zib saw Pepper.

"Oh!" said Zib. "A small dark tiger!"

Pepper jumped straight up in the air. Then she ran and hid inside one of Dad's rain boots. Only her tail was sticking out.

---

"That's Pepper," said Mari. "She's a cat. She's scared of everything. Don't worry, she's nice."

"Hello-treat, Pepper," said Zib to the boot.

The tail in the boot went swish, swish. Mari thought that was probably a hello.

Then Zib floated back to the pumpkin. It looked inside, and its antennae turned a sad gray color.

"What's wrong?" asked Mari.

"My ship," said Zib. "My pumpkin-ship. It is broken. Not broken-broken. Just… empty."

Mari looked inside the pumpkin too. It was not full of seeds and goo, like a real pumpkin. It had a little seat, and buttons, and a round glass jar in the middle. The jar had eight small holes in it, like an egg box. All eight holes were empty.

---

<!-- ilustracion: Close-up inside the pumpkin spaceship: a tiny cushioned seat, round glowing buttons and a clear glass jar in the middle with eight empty round holes like an egg carton; Zib's small purple hand pointing at the jar; Mari looking in over the edge. -->

"My ship needs glow-seeds," said Zib. "Eight of them. They are like… like batteries, but shiny. Very bright. They make the ship go zoom."

"Where are they?" asked Mari.

Zib made a sound like a sigh. "When I land, I land too fast. Bump! The jar opens, and the glow-seeds fly out. Pop, pop, pop! All over your town. In eight different places. I do not know where."

"Oh no," said Mari.

"Oh no, yes," said Zib. "And there is one more problem."

Zib pointed up at the sky again. In the morning light, Mari could just see the moon, pale and white, like a ghost of the moon.

"My ship can only fly home when the moon is round and full," said Zib. "Full moon makes a road. A road of light, all the way home."

---

Mari thought about what Abuela had said last night.

"The full moon is on Halloween!" said Mari. "That's in seven days."

"Seven days," said Zib, very quietly. "If I find all eight glow-seeds in seven days, I go home on Halloween night. My mama and my papa and my little brother will be so happy."

"And if you don't find them?"

Zib's antennae drooped. "Then I wait. One whole year, for the next good moon. I will be okay. But I will miss them very much."

Mari thought about missing Abuela, and Mom and Dad, and even Pepper, for a whole year. It made her tummy feel sad and heavy.

She stood up straight. She fixed her helmet.

---

<!-- ilustracion: Mari standing tall in her pajamas and cardboard helmet with a determined smile, one hand raised like a promise; Zib floating in front of her doing a happy flip, its three antennae glowing every color of the rainbow; the pumpkin spaceship open behind them in the morning garden. -->

"Zib," she said, "I will help you find your glow-seeds."

Zib's antennae lit up like a rainbow. Red, orange, yellow, green, blue and purple, all at once.

"You will? Really-really?"

"Really-really," said Mari. "Astronauts help each other. Even pretend ones."

"You are not pretend," said Zib. "You are the most real astronaut I know." Zib thought for a second. "You are the only astronaut I know. But still!"

Then Zib went very still. Its three antennae stood straight up, like the ears of a dog that hears a cookie bag.

The lights on the ends of the antennae turned yellow. And all three of them pointed the same way, across the yard, over the fence, down the street.

---

"What is it?" Mari whispered.

"A glow-seed," said Zib. "I can feel it. My antennae can feel them when they are close-ish. Yellow means close-ish."

Mari looked where the antennae were pointing. Past the fence. Past the big oak tree on the corner. Past the stop sign.

She knew that way. She walked that way every single morning with her backpack.

"That's the way to my school," said Mari.

Just then, from inside the house, Mom called out, "Mari! Breakfast! And put on your clothes, mija, or you'll be late for school!"

Mari looked at Zib. Zib looked at Mari.

"Zib," said Mari slowly, "how do you feel about backpacks?"
